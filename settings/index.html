Cleanup

Remove orphan thumbnails manually. They may pile up in case files get moved around or deleted on the system.

To automate orphan cleanup you can add this line to your cronjobs:
/usr/bin/curl -k https://[::1]/fancy-directory-index/settings/thumbnail.php?cleanup=1
